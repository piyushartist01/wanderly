Help Centre

Frequently asked questions and support.

What is your cancellation policy?
Most of our packages offer free cancellation up to 15 days before the departure date. For cancellations made within 15 days, a tiered fee applies. Please check the specific package details for exact terms.
Are flights included in the package price?
No, our base package prices do not include flights to the destination. We believe this gives you the flexibility to book flights using your preferred airline or miles. However, all internal transport during the trip is included.
Is travel insurance mandatory?
For trekking and high-altitude trips (like Ladakh or Kedarkantha), comprehensive travel insurance covering medical evacuation is mandatory. For regular cultural or beach trips, it is highly recommended but not mandatory.
How do I know if a trek is right for my fitness level?
Every package is tagged with a difficulty rating (Easy, Moderate, Challenging). We also provide a detailed itinerary with altitude and distance metrics. If you're unsure, you can contact our expert guides who will help assess if a trip is right for you.
